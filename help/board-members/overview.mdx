As a member of the Credentials Board, your role in Credentials Portal is to participate in the candidate interview process — reviewing candidate information, conducting interviews, and documenting your board's findings.

[Screenshot: Board member dashboard showing upcoming interviews and recent candidates]

## What you can do

  <Card title="Conduct Interviews" icon="clipboard-check" href="/help/board-members/interviews">
    Fill in interview forms, record audio, and attach supporting files.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Record & Transcribe Audio" icon="microphone" href="/help/board-members/audio">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Message Candidates & Mentors" icon="message" href="/help/messaging/conversations">

## What you can see

Your access is scoped to your assigned board and the candidates who appear before it:

| Data | Access |
|------|--------|
| People directory | View (read-only) |
| Interview records (your board) | View and edit |
| Interview private fields | Yes |
| Candidate profiles | View |
| Candidate files | View |
| District settings | No |
| Other boards' interviews | No (unless admin) |

## Your board assignment

Your district administrator assigns you to one or more boards. You can only view and conduct interviews for candidates appearing before your board(s).

If you believe you've been assigned to the wrong board, contact your district administrator.

## Getting started

1. Log in at [app.credentialsportal.com](https://app.credentialsportal.com) — see [Logging In](/help/getting-started/login) if you need help
2. Review candidates who are scheduled for your board
3. See [Conducting Interviews](/help/board-members/interviews) for step-by-step interview documentation instructions

## Questions?

Contact your district administrator, or reach out to [help@credentialsportal.com](mailto:help@credentialsportal.com).
