---
title: Managing People
description: Add, edit, invite, and organize the people in your district.
---

The People section is where you manage everyone in your district — candidates pursuing credentials, mentors guiding them, board members, and administrators.

[Screenshot: People list view showing names, roles, and status badges]

## Add a person

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open People">
    Click **People** in the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Person">
    Click the **+ Add Person** button in the top-right corner.

    [Screenshot: Add Person button in the People view]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in their details">
    Enter at minimum their first name, last name, and email address. You can also set their language preference and assign tags.

    [Screenshot: Add person form with name, email, and language fields]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign roles">
    Select one or more roles for this person. The role determines what they can see and do in the app.

    [Screenshot: Role selector showing Admin, Board, Mentor, and Candidate options]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The person is added to your district but can't log in until you send them an invitation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send an invitation

After adding a person, send them an invitation so they can access the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the person's profile">
    Click their name in the People list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invitation">
    Click **Send Invitation**. They'll receive an email with a login link.

    [Screenshot: Send Invitation button on a person's profile]
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
You can resend an invitation at any time from the person's profile if they didn't receive the first one.
</Tip>

## Edit a person

Click any person's name to open their profile. You can edit:

- Name, email, language
- Assigned roles, tracks, tags
- Custom fields (fields specific to their role)
- Profile photo

Click **Save** after making changes.

## Assign to a track

To track a candidate's credentialing progress, assign them to a track:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open their profile">
    Navigate to the candidate's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a track">
    In the **Track** field, choose the appropriate credentialing track (e.g., Ministerial Track, Transfer Track).

    [Screenshot: Track selector on a person's profile]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark course completions">
    As the candidate completes courses, check them off in their profile. Each completion is timestamped.

    [Screenshot: Course completion checklist with dates]
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assign a mentor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate's profile">
    Navigate to the candidate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the mentor">
    In the candidate's profile, select their mentor from the **Mentor** field.

    [Screenshot: Mentor selector on a candidate's profile]
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Archive a person

Archiving removes someone from the active People list without permanently deleting their records.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open their profile">
    Navigate to the person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive">
    Click the **Archive** option in the profile menu. Archived people can't log in.

    [Screenshot: Archive option in the person profile action menu]
  </Step>
</Steps>

To view archived people, toggle the **Show Archived** filter in the People list.

## Delete a person

Deleting permanently removes a person and all their associated data (interviews, files, messages).

<Warning>
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Archive a person instead if you may need their records later.
</Warning>

Open their profile, click the profile action menu, and select **Delete**.

## Filter and search

Use the search bar to find people by name. Use the filter options to narrow by role, track, or tag.
