<Note>
Billing is accessible to users with the **Billing** permission on their role (Admin role by default).
</Note>

## Trial period

New districts start with a **30-day free trial** — no credit card required. During the trial you have access to all features with no restrictions.

When your trial ends, you'll need an active subscription to continue using the app. A banner will appear in the app as your trial approaches its end date.

[Screenshot: Trial expiration banner showing days remaining]

## Activate a subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Billing">
    Click **Settings** in the navigation, then select **Billing**.

    [Screenshot: Billing settings page showing trial status and plan options]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select the plan that fits your district's size and needs. Plan details and pricing are shown on the billing page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payment details">
    Click **Subscribe** to be taken to a secure Stripe checkout page. Enter your credit card information.

    [Screenshot: Stripe checkout page with card entry form]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    After successful payment, your district is activated and billing status updates immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage your subscription

Once subscribed, click **Manage Billing** on the Billing page to open the Stripe customer portal. From there you can:

- View invoices and payment history
- Update your payment method
- Change or cancel your plan

[Screenshot: Billing page showing the Manage Billing button]

## What happens if a subscription lapses

If your subscription expires or a payment fails, access to the app is restricted until your subscription is updated. Your data is retained — you won't lose records — but members won't be able to log in or use the app until billing is resolved.
