As a mentor, your role is to guide candidates through the credentialing process — offering support, reviewing their progress, and staying informed about their board interviews.

[Screenshot: Mentor dashboard showing assigned candidates with their track progress indicators]

## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="View Candidate Progress" icon="chart-line" href="/help/mentors/candidates">
    See your candidates' course completions, track progress, and interview history.
  </Card>
  <Card title="View Published Interviews" icon="clipboard-check" href="/help/mentors/candidates">
    Read published interview results for candidates assigned to you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Message Candidates" icon="message" href="/help/messaging/conversations">
    Send direct or group messages to your candidates and other district members.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Update Your Profile" icon="user" href="/help/profile/edit-profile">
    Edit your name, language preference, and profile photo.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What you can see

| Data | Access |
|------|--------|
| Your assigned candidates' profiles | View |
| Published interviews (your candidates) | View (no private fields) |
| Candidates' course progress | View |
| Candidates' files | View |
| District settings | No |
| Other candidates (not assigned to you) | No |

## Who your candidates are

Your district administrator assigns candidates to you. You can see your assigned candidates in the **People** directory — they'll be listed as your mentees.

If you're not seeing any candidates, contact your district administrator to confirm your assignments.

## Getting started

1. Log in at [app.credentialsportal.com](https://app.credentialsportal.com)
2. Go to **People** to see your assigned candidates
3. See [Viewing Candidate Progress](/help/mentors/candidates) for a full guide

## Questions?

Contact your district administrator, or reach out to [help@credentialsportal.com](mailto:help@credentialsportal.com).
