---
title: Editing Your Profile
description: Update your name, language, and profile photo in Credentials Portal.
---

Your profile stores the basic information that appears throughout the app — in messages, interview records, and the people directory.

[Screenshot: Profile page showing name, email, language, and avatar fields]

## Update your information

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click your avatar or name in the top-right corner of the app, then select **Profile**.

    [Screenshot: Account menu with Profile option highlighted]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your details">
    Update your first name, last name, or preferred language. Your email address is managed by your district administrator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click **Save** to apply your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change your profile photo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Navigate to your profile as described above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a photo">
    Click your current avatar (or the avatar placeholder) and select a photo from your device. Supported formats: JPEG, PNG.

    [Screenshot: Avatar upload area with click-to-upload prompt]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Crop and confirm">
    Use the crop tool to frame your photo, then click **Save**.

    [Screenshot: Photo cropping interface]
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change your language

Your language setting controls:
- The language the app interface is displayed in
- The language used when messages are auto-translated to you

To change it, go to your profile and select a language from the **Language** dropdown.

<Tip>
Credentials Portal supports 90+ languages. Messages sent by others are automatically translated into your selected language.
</Tip>

## What you can't change yourself

| Field | Who can change it |
|-------|------------------|
| Email address | District administrator |
| Assigned roles | District administrator |
| Track enrollment | District administrator |

Contact your district administrator for changes to these fields, or reach out to [help@credentialsportal.com](mailto:help@credentialsportal.com) if you need further assistance.
