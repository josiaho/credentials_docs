Credentials Portal helps districts track the full credentialing journey: from a candidate's first course enrollment through their board interviews, mentor relationship, and final recommendation.

[Screenshot: Credentials Portal dashboard showing the main navigation with People, Interviews, Messages, and Courses sections]

## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card icon="users" href="/help/admin/people" title="Manage People">
    Add candidates, mentors, and board members. Assign roles, tracks, and tags.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="clipboard-check" href="/help/board-members/interviews" title="Track Interviews">
    Schedule and document board interviews with structured forms, audio recording, and file attachments.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="chart-line" href="/help/candidates/progress" title="Monitor Progress">
    Candidates and mentors can see course completion, track progress, and upcoming requirements.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="message" href="/help/messaging/conversations" title="Communicate">
    Send direct messages, group conversations, and district-wide broadcasts.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who is this guide for?

Use the sidebar to jump to help content written for your role:

| Role             | Description                                                              |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Admin**        | District administrators who manage people, settings, and billing         |
| **Board Member** | Credentials committee members who conduct and document interviews        |
| **Mentor**       | Mentors assigned to guide candidates through their credentialing journey |
| **Candidate**    | Ministers working toward credentials through a specific track            |

<Note>
  Your district administrator determines which features are available to you based on your assigned role.
</Note>
