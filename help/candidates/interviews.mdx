Your interview records capture what happened at your credentialing board meeting — the committee's written feedback, their recommendation, attached files, and any audio recordings made available to you.

[Screenshot: Candidate interview view showing a published interview with recommendation, feedback, and an audio player]

## View your interviews

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Interviews">
    Click **Interviews** in the main navigation, or find the Interviews section on your profile.

    [Screenshot: Interviews list showing interview dates and statuses]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a published interview">
    Published interviews are available to view. Unpublished (pending) interviews are not visible.

    [Screenshot: Interview list with a "Published" badge on a completed interview]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    The interview record shows:
    - The committee's **recommendation** (e.g., Recommend / Do not recommend)
    - **Written feedback** from the board
    - Any **files** the board attached
    - **Audio recording** (if recorded and made available)

    [Screenshot: Published interview record with all visible sections labeled]
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Interview status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Interview created but results not yet published — not visible to you |
| **Published** | Results finalized and available for you to view |

## Understanding the feedback

The board's written feedback is meant to help you grow. Common fields include:

- **What the committee appreciated** about your interview
- **Areas to work on** — suggestions for continued development
- **Committee recommendation** — whether the board recommends you for credentials at this time

<Note>
Some fields are marked "private" and visible only to district administrators. You will not see these, even in published interviews. This is by design and does not affect your access to the publicly-shared feedback.
</Note>

## Audio recordings

If the board recorded the interview and the administrator has made it available, you'll see an audio player in the interview record. You can:

- Listen to the recording
- Read the AI-generated transcript
- Generate an AI summary of the transcript on demand

[Screenshot: Audio player section with transcript and summary below it]

## Notifications

You'll receive a notification (email and/or push, based on your preferences) when an interview result is published. See [Notification Preferences](/help/profile/notification-preferences) to manage how you're notified.

## Questions about your results?

Contact your mentor or district administrator for guidance on your interview results.
